Study Tool 1.3.1

All arrowheads now appear in the middle of connecting lines, keeping them clear of card endpoints. This covers ordinary arrows, double-line implication arrows and equivalence connections, pointing forward, backward or both ways. Two-way heads sit together at the centre.

Heads follow the tangent halfway along curved lines and stay in place as you move cards, pan or zoom. Selected connections retain their highlight. Short connections keep their intended direction. Plain lines remain available.

The change applies to existing connections automatically in both Edit and Display. Saved flowcharts, connection styles, LaTeX settings, backups, workspace format and save location remain compatible.

All 72 automated checks pass, including midpoint geometry, every connection style, movement, undo, persistence, storage and update safeguards. Both Apple Silicon and Intel Mac editions are built and verified by the release workflow.
